- (requirement) R1 (FR-1 [AUTH-01]) An Applicant submits an Account Request (student number, full name, email, course, year level, section, password and confirmation; optional mobile). Missing fields, year level <= 0, malformed email, mismatched confirmation, or an email/student number used by a pending request, Account or Member Profile is refused with field errors. Password kept only as a hash; Applicant sees an acknowledgment only and cannot choose Role or membership values.
- (requirement) R2 (FR-2 [AUTH-02]) Management approves or rejects a pending Account Request. Approval atomically creates an active Member Account and its Member Profile, moves the hash, re-checks email and student-number uniqueness, and is audited. Rejection needs a reason, clears the hash and creates no Account. Concurrent decisions yield one outcome; a finalized request cannot be reviewed again.
- (requirement) R3 (FR-3 [AUTH-03]) An active Account logs in with its email and password and logs out. Every failure is generic. Success issues a signed, expiring cookie session routed by Role. Every protected request re-checks current Account active state and Role.
- (requirement) R4 (FR-4 [MD-01]) A Member views their own account, academic and membership details; nothing is editable.
- (requirement) R5 (FR-5 [MD-02]) Management edits a Member's full name, course, year level, section, mobile, membership type and membership status. A student-number correction requires a reason, stays unique and logs old/new values. Login email is read-only. Edits never rewrite Registration snapshots or issued Certificates.
- (requirement) R6 (FR-6 [MD-03]) Management creates, edits, activates and deactivates Event Types with a unique, non-blank name and a non-blank description. Inactive types cannot be chosen for new or draft Events; existing references are kept.
- (requirement) R7 (FR-7 [MD-04]) Management maintains Venues: room name, positive capacity, location, availability notes, contact person, active state and a required photo. Capacity never drops below the participant limit of a linked draft or published Event. Inactive Venues cannot be chosen for new or draft Events.
- (requirement) R8 (FR-8 [MD-05]) Management uploads or replaces a Venue photo or Event Cover: JPEG/PNG verified by content, at most 2 MiB, server-generated filename, one current image per record. A failed file or database write leaves the previous image intact.
- (requirement) R9 (FR-9 [EVT-01, EVT-03]) Management creates and edits draft Events: title, description, active Event Type, active Venue, start, participant limit > 0 and <= Venue capacity, Signup Deadline <= start, optional Cover. Drafts are hidden from Members.
- (requirement) R10 (FR-10 [EVT-01, EVT-02]) Management publishes a draft only with an active type and venue (revalidated), a Cover, a valid capacity and a future Signup Deadline. Publication is audited, shows the Event in the Member feed and opens signup at once.
- (requirement) R11 (FR-11 [EVT-03]) On a Published Event only title, description and Cover can change, with old/new values logged. Completed and Cancelled Events are read-only.
- (requirement) R12 (FR-12 [EVT-04]) Management cancels a Draft or Published Event that has no Valid Attendance and no issued Certificate, with a reason. Cancellation atomically cancels its pending, confirmed and waitlisted Registrations, invalidates their QR Passes, closes Check-in and is audited; no Promotion.
- (requirement) R13 (FR-13 [EVT-04]) Management completes a Published Event at or after its start. Completion closes Check-in and creates a Certificate Review for every Registration with Valid Attendance. Nothing completes an Event automatically; completion before start is refused.
- (requirement) R14 (FR-14 [REG-01]) An Eligible Participant registers for a Published Event before its Signup Deadline, creating a pending Registration with a course/year/section snapshot. A second Registration for the same Member and Event is refused, even after cancellation. Full Events accept signup with a no-seat-guaranteed warning. Overlapping Events are allowed.
- (requirement) R15 (FR-15 [EVT-02, REG-05]) At or after the Signup Deadline the server refuses new signups, stale forms included; the Event stays Published and existing Registrations are unaffected.
- (requirement) R16 (FR-16 [REG-02]) Before the Event Start Cutoff Management approves or declines (reason required) Pending Registrations, and administratively cancels Confirmed or Waitlisted ones without Valid Attendance (reason required, QR invalidated, Seat released, no Promotion). Approval confirms only when a Seat is free and no earlier eligible Waitlisted Registration has priority; otherwise it waitlists. Concurrent last-seat approvals never overbook. Confirmation generates a unique, unpredictable QR token.
- (requirement) R17 (FR-17 [REG-04]) Before the Event Start Cutoff Management promotes the earliest Waitlisted Registration whose owner is still an Eligible Participant (by review time, then registration ID, across the whole queue) when a Seat is free; otherwise nothing changes. Promotion is serialized with approvals and never automatic.
- (requirement) R18 (FR-18 [REG-03]) After an explicit confirmation, a Member cancels their own pending, confirmed or waitlisted Registration on a Published Event before the Event Start Cutoff and before Valid Attendance. The QR Pass is invalidated, the Seat released, no Promotion follows, and re-registration is refused.
- (requirement) R19 (FR-19 [REG-05]) At or after the Event start the server refuses reviews, confirmations, Promotions and member cancellations. Pending and Waitlisted rows stay unchanged and show as Not confirmed before cutoff; they are not No-shows.
- (requirement) R20 (FR-20 [REG-01, ATT-03]) A Member sees all their Registrations with status, the QR Pass when Confirmed, Attendance Record state including invalidation, and download links for issued Certificates.
- (requirement) R21 (FR-21 [QR-01]) The owner of a Confirmed Registration views its QR Pass and downloads it as a PNG generated on demand. The QR encodes only the token. Ownership is checked; cancelled or unconfirmed Registrations have no usable pass.
- (requirement) R22 (FR-22 [ATT-01]) Management opens and closes Check-in for a Published Event at any time, each toggle audited. Completion and cancellation close it; nothing opens it automatically.
- (requirement) R23 (FR-23 [ATT-01]) Management scans a QR Pass. A valid scan records one Attendance Record (method, recorder, UTC time) and shows name and student number. A repeat scan shows the original time and inserts nothing. Wrong-event, ineligible, cancelled, unconfirmed, invalidated, closed or unpublished, and unknown tokens are rejected without inserting or revealing other records.
- (requirement) R24 (FR-24 [ATT-01]) Management finds a Confirmed participant by name or student number and checks them in under the same validations as a scan, recording manual_search or student_number. Searching, viewing or downloading never records attendance.
- (requirement) R25 (FR-25 [ATT-02]) Management invalidates an Attendance Record after a confirmation and reason, keeping the row. It leaves reports and eligibility, turns any unissued Certificate Review not_eligible, is blocked once a Certificate is issued, and is serialized against issuance. Re-check-in after invalidation is refused.
- (requirement) R26 (FR-26 [ATT-03]) A Member sees their own Attendance Records with invalidated ones marked, and cannot check in or correct themselves.
- (requirement) R27 (FR-27 [CERT-01]) Management approves (eligible) or declines (not_eligible, reason required, final) each pending Certificate Review, recording reviewer and time. Approval re-checks Valid Attendance, serialized against invalidation.
- (requirement) R28 (FR-28 [CERT-02]) Management issues one eligible Certificate at a time: a landscape A4 PDF from one fixed template with embedded fonts, a unique certificate number, and snapshots of recipient name, Event title and start, issue date and issuer. Issuance re-checks approval, Valid Attendance and completion; duplicate issuance is impossible; a failed write leaves it unissued; a retry never replaces an issued PDF; success shows only after the final commit.
- (requirement) R29 (FR-29 [CERT-02]) The active Account owner or Management downloads an issued Certificate streamed as application/pdf through an endpoint that checks permission per request, never as a static file or caller-supplied path. An inactive Account is blocked; inactive membership is not. Later edits never alter the retained PDF.
- (requirement) R30 (FR-30 [REP-01]) Management views per-Event participation: Registrations by status, Confirmed and Valid attendees, No-shows (Completed Events only), attendance rate (N/A with no Confirmed), attendee course/year distribution from signup snapshots, and detail rows (name, student number, course, registration status, attendance result, check-in time). Totals cover the full dataset; inactivity never hides history.
- (requirement) R31 (FR-31 [ADM-01]) An Administrator assigns the Member, Officer or Administrator Role to an Account. The last active Administrator cannot be demoted. Roles are fixed.
- (requirement) R32 (FR-32 [ADM-01]) Before a membership-status or Account-access deactivation the screen previews affected upcoming Registrations and requires a reason. Deactivation cancels the person's Registrations on not-yet-started Events that lack Valid Attendance, invalidates their QR Passes and releases Seats without Promotion; Account deactivation ends existing sessions; the last active Administrator cannot be deactivated. Reactivation restores access only; issued Certificates are kept.
- (requirement) R33 (FR-33 [ADM-02]) Every consequential action FR-33 lists appends one Activity Log entry (actor, action, entity type and ID, reason, UTC time, validated old/new JSON) in the same commit as the change. Entries never hold passwords, hashes, QR tokens or file paths, and are never updated or deleted.
- (requirement) R34 (FR-34 [ADM-02]) An Administrator views a read-only, newest-first Activity Log showing actor, action, affected record, time and reason.
- (requirement) R35 (FR-35) A one-time setup script creates the first active Administrator from a supplied name, email and password stored as a hash; no default credentials exist and public requests never grant privileged Roles.
- (requirement) R36 (FR-36) Every list screen FR-36 names shows 25 rows per page with Previous/Next, deterministic ordering with a unique-ID tie-break, filters and authorization applied before counting, missing/invalid pages showing page 1 and out-of-range pages the last page, hidden navigation on one page, and an empty state.
