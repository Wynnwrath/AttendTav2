# Requirements Lens Review — AttendTa UX

## Summary

The spines preserve most major PRD flows and correctly defer to the PRD/SPEC. Five implementation-facing issues remain: one competing-review outcome conflicts with the requirements, one generic dialog rule contradicts optional cancellation reasons, and three state/decision distinctions need clearer UX treatment.

## Findings

- **High — Competing last-seat approval has the wrong failure outcome.** `EXPERIENCE.md:106` says competing reviews may be “rejected or returned with the current outcome.” The PRD says exactly one last-seat approval succeeds and the other becomes Waitlisted (`../prd-attendta/prd-attendta.md:61`); FR-16 says an approval without a free seat or with an earlier eligible waitlisted participant becomes Waitlisted (`../prd-attendta/prd-attendta.md:252-254`), consistent with SPEC §9.2 (`../../../docsss/AttendTa-System-Specification.md:194`). *Fix:* Make the competing approval's visible outcome Waitlisted, with current status and next step; reserve rejection for an action that is no longer valid.

- **High — Membership inactivity and account deactivation are not distinguished in the experience.** The access/admin pattern groups affected-registration preview and reason together (`EXPERIENCE.md:64`), while the IA lists member maintenance separately from role/access management without stating the different consequences (`EXPERIENCE.md:24-30`). The PRD requires membership inactivity to block participation while leaving account access intact, and account deactivation to block access (`../prd-attendta/prd-attendta.md:158`, `407-415`). SPEC confirms the distinction and preserves personal history/certificate access for membership-inactive members (`../../../docsss/AttendTa-System-Specification.md:118-126`). *Fix:* Specify separate controls and confirmation summaries: membership status blocks participation but preserves account/history access; account deactivation blocks access. Show the applicable upcoming-registration effects and state that no waitlist promotion occurs.

- **Medium — The dialog rule makes member cancellation require a reason.** `EXPERIENCE.md:51` says consequential actions have a “required reason.” Member cancellation only has an optional reason (`../prd-attendta/prd-attendta.md:269-271`; SPEC §9.4, `../../../docsss/AttendTa-System-Specification.md:202`). *Fix:* Scope required reasons to actions that require them; keep member-cancellation reason optional while retaining the final-cancellation warning.

- **Medium — The finality of certificate declines is not surfaced before the decision.** The certificate flow permits approving or declining each review but does not call out that decline is irreversible (`EXPERIENCE.md:117-124`); certificate states likewise omit the no-reopen rule (`EXPERIENCE.md:65`). The PRD requires a warning before decline and says it is final (`../prd-attendta/prd-attendta.md:352-355`); SPEC defers reopening and requires the warning (`../../../docsss/AttendTa-System-Specification.md:268-270`). *Fix:* Add a pre-confirmation warning that a declined review cannot be reopened in this release, alongside the required reason.

- **Low — “Not confirmed before cutoff” reads like a separate registration status.** `EXPERIENCE.md:62` lists it beside Pending, Confirmed, Waitlisted and Cancelled. The PRD defines it as the display label for Pending or Waitlisted after event start (`../prd-attendta/prd-attendta.md:91`, `277`); SPEC says those underlying rows remain unchanged (`../../../docsss/AttendTa-System-Specification.md:206`). *Fix:* Describe it as a derived label on Pending/Waitlisted rows, preserving their stored status and excluding them from no-show counts.
