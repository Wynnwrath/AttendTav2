# PRD Quality Review — AttendTa

> **Historical snapshot (2026-11-04).** This review predates the latest owner-confirmed decisions and source-document alignment. Treat its findings as historical; the current `prd-attendta.md`, `addendum.md`, and current `docsss/` sources govern.

## Overall verdict
The PRD is well anchored. Its thesis ("explicit and conservative") is enforced by the features, the Recorded Decisions table (§9) names each source conflict, and the attendance, Waitlist and report rules (FR-16, FR-17, FR-23, FR-30) are specific enough to test. What is at risk: three core rules a builder would have to guess (who cancels Registrations on deactivation, what "eligible" means for Promotion, how a mistyped login email is corrected); a §1 scope claim that §7.1 contradicts, with the schedule risk kept out of the PRD body; and a length and security-bounds gap against the stated 5–8 page target. After a focused edit pass it can drive story creation; it is not yet a green-light document.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
The PRD is honest about source conflicts: §9 lists eleven disagreements with a decision each, §10 gives owners to Q1, Q2 and Q4, and the FR-23 table resolves every scan outcome. The gaps are tensions it has not surfaced. Deactivation consequences in FR-5 and FR-32 contradict each other and UJ-5. Proxy check-in is accepted in NFR-6 and UJ-3 without naming the cost that addendum §E names. A mistyped email has no recovery path, and the PRD never states that as a trade-off. The certificate self-approval question in FR-27 is tagged as an assumption rather than raised as a decision. Finally, §1 claims the scope is "deliberately reduced", while §7.1 lists all 26 IDs; the cut order is left to the PM through the single NOTE FOR PM in §7.2.

### Findings
- **high** Deactivation consequences ambiguous between membership and Account (§4.2 FR-5; §4.8 FR-32; UJ-5). FR-5 says a membership change "blocks new participation. It does not change Account access, and Account access changes do not change membership," and says nothing about existing Registrations. FR-32 opens "Management can change membership status. An Administrator can change Account active state," then writes one consequence bullet, "For Events not yet started, deactivation cancels the person's pending, waitlisted and confirmed Registrations…", that could apply to either action. UJ-5 shows Account deactivation cancelling Registrations. The PRD never says which action cancels Registrations. *Fix:* Split the FR-32 consequences by action. State whether membership deactivation cancels Registrations and whether Account deactivation does, then align FR-5 and UJ-5 with that.
- **high** Shared or offline QR Pass proxy check-in is not named as a trade-off (§4.5 FR-21, FR-23; §5 NFR-6; UJ-3). FR-21 allows PNG download, NFR-6 says "Members may show an offline saved QR Pass", and FR-23 accepts any valid token. The only check the screen gives is a name and student number. Addendum §E states the risk ("A screenshot of a QR Pass does not prove identity, so officer verification is still needed"), but the PRD body does not. §1 calls check-in "duplicate-proof" and §2.1 promises "credible attendance numbers", and both depend on this check. *Fix:* State the risk in FR-23 or NFR-6 and make officer visual verification part of the check-in rule. Record the accepted trade-off in §9, or raise it as a NOTE FOR PM.
- **high** No correction path for a mistyped login email (§4.1 FR-1, FR-3; §4.2 FR-5; §6). FR-5 says "Login email is read-only for everyone." §6 excludes "email verification, password reset or recovery, self-service email or password change", and NFR-3 forbids hard deletes. An Applicant who mistypes the email in FR-1 cannot get back to a usable Account, and the PRD does not say so. *Fix:* Either add a Management-only, reason-logged email correction to FR-5, or record the lockout as an accepted consequence in §6 and §9, with its workaround.
- **high** §1 scope claim contradicts §7.1, and schedule risk is kept out of the PRD body (§1 "The scope is deliberately reduced so a five-person student team can deliver a correct, demonstrable MVP"; §7.1; §7.2 NOTE FOR PM; §10 Q2). §7.1 includes all 26 functional IDs plus FR-35 and FR-36. Addendum §E rates "Broad scope with unfinalized edge cases" as High probability, and addendum §C gives 46 MD / 2.3 MM against a baseline the PRD says is stale. The PRD body states no feasibility basis, and the NOTE FOR PM leaves the cut order undecided. *Fix:* Replace "deliberately reduced" with an accurate description of scope. Put the effort basis in §7 or §10. Record the cut order, or state that none has been set.
- **medium** Certificate self-approval is tagged as an assumption, not surfaced as a tension (§4.6 FR-27; §11). FR-27 lets "Officers" review "their own participation" for the credential that proves attendance. The tag reads "no self-approval prohibition, per SPEC §2", which asserts that a prohibition is absent rather than recording a decision. *Fix:* Convert it to a NOTE FOR PM that states the choice and its consequence, or confirm it and record it in §9.

## Substance over theater — strong
The Vision (§1) is specific to this product. It names the QR Pass, the Waitlist, certificate eligibility and the "explicit and conservative" character, and §4 and §6 enforce that character. There is no innovation section. NFRs carry product bounds: NFR-4 requires storage to survive redeploy, NFR-6 gives 1920×1080, and NFR-3 sets retention. The five JTBD entries in §2.1 are four users and one social stakeholder, and each drives a rule (FR-30 for the social one). The theater risk here is length, not padding, and is covered under Shape fit. Security NFR text is partly generic; that is a bounds gap, covered under Done-ness.

### Findings
- **low** Five JTBD entries, one of them a non-user stakeholder (§2.1 "Organization (social)"). It drives FR-30 so it stays, but the list reads as five personas when the set is four users plus one stakeholder. *Fix:* Label it as a stakeholder line, or fold it into the Vision.

## Strategic coherence — strong
The thesis "explicit and conservative" holds through the features. Waitlist promotion is manual (FR-17, §1), completion is an explicit action (FR-13), and every consequential action is meant to be logged (FR-33). §6 Non-Goals repeats the stance, and SM-C1 and SM-C2 are named counter-metrics tied to SM-1 and SM-2. Success metrics are acceptance-based, as the recorded decision requires. The one mismatch, the §1 "deliberately reduced" sentence, is recorded as a High finding under Decision-readiness.

### Findings
None beyond the §1 scope mismatch above.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Most FRs have testable consequences. Examples are FR-16 (concurrent last seat never overbooks), the FR-23 scan table, FR-30 (N/A on zero denominator), FR-36 (page edge cases) and FR-8 (2 MiB, content-verified, failed write keeps the prior image). The gaps are specific. Promotion eligibility is undefined. Security NFRs use adjectives. "Fast" and "credible" are unbounded. The FR-33 log list is narrower than the Vision promises. Two FRs have no consequences.

### Findings
- **high** Promotion eligibility is undefined (§4.4 FR-16, FR-17). FR-16 requires "no earlier eligible Waitlisted Registration has priority", and FR-17 requires that the chosen Registration "must still be eligible". In §3, "eligible" is defined only for Certificate Review. FR-23 lists eligibility rules for check-in, which do not transfer. A builder cannot write the candidate filter for FR-17 from the PRD. *Fix:* Add an explicit Promotion eligibility rule to §3 and cite it in FR-16 and FR-17. For example: Account active, Member Profile active, Event Published and before the Event Start Cutoff, Registration still waitlisted.
- **medium** Authentication and session bounds are missing (§5 NFR-1; §4.1 FR-1, FR-3; Glossary QR Pass). NFR-1 says "Signed, expiring cookie sessions" with no lifetime. No minimum password rule appears anywhere. FR-3 has no login throttling or lockout, although student numbers are guessable and FR-3 uses them as a login identifier. The QR token is "unpredictable" with no entropy bound. *Fix:* State the session lifetime, a password minimum, a login-attempt limit and the token entropy, or point to the addendum with the number the addendum chooses.
- **medium** Adjectives and unbounded claims stand in for requirements (§1 "Check-in becomes fast"; §2.1 "get in quickly on the day" and "credible attendance numbers"; FR-10 "a valid capacity"; FR-28 "the issuer"). No performance or capacity NFR exists to bound "fast". The certificate's issuer and the certificate number format are both undefined. *Fix:* Bound check-in response time, or remove "fast". Define "valid capacity" by reference to FR-9. Define the issuer (organization or officer) and the certificate number format.
- **medium** Activity Log coverage is narrower than the Vision promise (§1 "every consequential action is attributable and retained"; §3 Activity Log; FR-33). FR-33 is a closed list. It omits Event Type and Venue create, edit and deactivate (FR-6, FR-7), Account Request submission (FR-1), Certificate downloads (FR-29) and initial setup (FR-35). Whether each is consequential is a decision the PRD should make. *Fix:* Add the missing actions to FR-33, or narrow the §1 claim and list what is excluded.
- **low** FR-4 and FR-35 have no testable consequences (§4.2, §4.8). FR-4 is a description only. FR-35 does not say what a second run of the setup script does. *Fix:* Add two or three consequences to each, for example "a second run is refused" and "no Role input is accepted".

## Scope honesty — strong
Non-Goals (§6) are specific and name the exclusion, for example "no outgoing email (Brevo), SMS or push". §7.2 gives a reason for every deferral. Open items are proportionate for a draft whose Q1 and Q2 are listed as blocking. There are 6 Open Questions, 4 inline ASSUMPTION tags and 1 NOTE FOR PM. Real tensions, namely FR-5 against FR-32, the §6 recovery gap and FR-27, are the places where a callout is missing. Those are raised as findings under Decision-readiness.

### Findings
- **medium** Assumption tagging does not round-trip for NFR-6 (§5 NFR-6; §11). NFR-6 states "layouts are mobile-responsive so check-in works on an officer's phone" as a requirement with no inline tag. §11 indexes "Mobile-responsive is assumed sufficient for phone-based check-in" as an assumption. *Fix:* Tag NFR-6 inline with the same text as the index entry.
- **low** §10 Q6 is an assumption presented as a question and answers itself: "This PRD reads it as Certificate owner plus Officer/Administrator `[ASSUMPTION]`. Confirm." The inline marker has no text. *Fix:* Move it out of §10 and keep it as a complete tag, for example `[ASSUMPTION: "Owner/Management" in CERT-02 means the Certificate owner plus Management.]`. Raise a real question in §10 only if one remains.

## Downstream usability — adequate
Strong points: FR-1 to FR-36 are contiguous with no duplicates. All 26 functional IDs are cited by at least one FR. UJ-1 to UJ-5 each realize a feature area, and each has a named protagonist. The glossary (§3) is present and mostly used verbatim. Weak points: the term "eligible" (see Done-ness), mixed audit vocabulary, undefined "signup" beside "Registration", and FR-17 naming database columns.

### Findings
- **low** Glossary drift across §1, §3, §4 and the UJs. The examples are: "Confirmed" and "confirmed" (FR-23 "Registration Confirmed", FR-30 "confirmed participants"); "audit entry" (FR-12) and "audit trail" (§1) against "Activity Log entry" (FR-2, §3); "signup" and "signups" (FR-14, FR-15) against "Registration", with no glossary entry; "cleared" (FR-21, "clears the token") against "invalidates" (FR-12, FR-18). *Fix:* Add "signup" to §3 or replace it with "Registration". Use "Activity Log entry" throughout. Use "invalidate" for QR Passes throughout.
- **low** Implementation detail appears in requirement text (FR-17 "`reviewed_at`, then `registration_id`"; FR-29 "`application/pdf`"; FR-33 "validated JSON old/new values"; FR-19 "times stored in UTC"). Shape fit flags these again. *Fix:* Restate each as the business rule, for example "earliest review time, then registration ID", and move the mechanism to the addendum.

## Shape fit — adequate
The product is multi-stakeholder with meaningful UX across four roles, so UJs are load-bearing, and the PRD has five, with edge cases. Most FRs cite their functional ID, which fits the stated chain-top role (UX, architecture, stories). The shape is over-formalized for the stated stakes. The stake is an academic project with a target of 5–8 pages. [INFERENCE] The file is 502 lines and roughly 6,000–7,000 words (36 FRs with consequence bullets, a 25-entry glossary and several tables). That is likely well over 8 pages. The file also carries addendum-level mechanics, as noted under Downstream usability.

### Findings
- **medium** Length exceeds the agreed 5–8 page target, and addendum-level detail sits in the PRD body (whole document; examples in FR-17, FR-19, FR-29, FR-33 and NFR-1). *Fix:* Move the mechanism-level detail to addendum.md. Compress the duplicated timing rules across FR-14, FR-15, FR-16 and FR-19. Trim the consequence bullets in FR-36 and FR-33 to their testable core.
